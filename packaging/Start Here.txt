LvPlan Pro - Start Here
==========================

WINDOWS COMPUTER (easiest)
  Double-click "LvPlan Pro.exe" in this folder.
  Your projects are saved right here on the flash drive (in "RadioPlan Data"),
  so you can plug the drive into any Windows 10 or 11 computer and keep working.

  To install it on the computer instead: double-click "RadioPlan-Pro-Setup.exe"
  (if it is in this folder) or download it from the LvPlan Pro update page.
  It installs by itself, puts an icon on the desktop, and keeps itself up to date.

MAC
  Double-click "Start on Mac.command". (The first time, right-click it and choose Open.)
  Needs Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge installed.

ANY OTHER COMPUTER
  Double-click "RadioPlan.html". It opens in your web browser.

Nothing else needs to be installed. Maps and terrain download by themselves when
there is internet and are kept for use without internet.

If Windows says "Windows protected your PC": click "More info", then "Run anyway".
This appears because the app is new and not yet signed with a paid certificate.
